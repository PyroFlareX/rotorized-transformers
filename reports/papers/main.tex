\begin{abstract}
This paper studies whether learned compositions of deterministic Givens rotations and diagonal scalings can represent Transformer feed-forward projection weights compactly. The central comparison is at equal stored bytes against ordinary low-rank and structured baselines; computational cost is reported separately. No experimental results are available yet.
\end{abstract}
